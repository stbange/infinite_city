# Design: add-pocket-doorways

## Context

The city streams around the camera with a floating origin (archived `add-world-streaming`);
blocks and bridges are generated by `CityLayout`. Requirements: the four spec deltas of this
change. Portal rendering must work in the Compatibility renderer.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A seamless "bigger on the inside" effect: live view, no loading cut, both directions.
- All rules (placement, interior size, pose mapping, crossing) are pure and unit-tested.

**Non-Goals:**
- Oblique near-plane clipping, recursive portals, several simultaneous live portals.
- Collision with interior walls (the camera is a free-flying spectator).

## Decisions

### D1 — Doors as transforms; pairing is one matrix (`src/core/portal_math.gd`)
A door is a `Transform3D` whose origin is the bottom centre of the opening, +Y up and +Z pointing
out of its front (the side you approach from). For doors A and B:
`M_AB = B · R_y(π) · A⁻¹`, and `M_BA = M_AB⁻¹`. A pose in front of A (local z > 0) maps behind B
(local z < 0) and vice versa, so the portal camera for "looking through A" is `M_AB · camera`.
`crossed(door, size, from, to)` works in door-local space: front-to-back sign change of z, with
the intersection point inside |x| ≤ w/2, 0 ≤ y ≤ h.

### D2 — `PocketLayout` decides doorways and interiors (`src/core/pocket_layout.gd`)
Wraps a `CityLayout`. `doorway_of(cell)`: transit hubs always, else
`u(seed, SALT_DOORWAY, cell) < 0.10`. Placement prefers the block's bridges (sorted by axis, then
owner cell) with room above the deck; otherwise a hashed face, lateral position and height.
Door transforms are stored relative to the cell centre (like block geometry), as `DoorwayInfo`.
`pocket_of(doorway)` returns a `PocketSpaceInfo`: kind from the block function, size
W ∈ [160, 320], D ∈ [240, 480], H ∈ [60, 140] m (≥ 14× the largest possible block), the inner
door at `(0, 0, -D/2)` facing +Z, and a seed for contents. Contents (pillars, stalls, trees,
light panels, a central monument) are expanded from that seed by `pocket_contents()` so they
are deterministic and testable.

### D3 — Out of sight, one at a time (`src/world/pocket_manager.gd`)
`PocketManager` picks the active doorway each frame (inside → that one; else nearest
`DoorwayView` within 160 m). It instantiates one `PocketInterior` placed so its inner door is at
`outer door + (0, 12 000, 0)` in scene space, with the same yaw as the outer door. The interior
joins the `world_anchored` group so rebasing moves it. 12 km keeps it beyond the 3 km far plane
of both views (spec: Out of sight) while keeping float32 error below 1 mm.
*Alternative:* integer "pocket realm" coordinates per doorway cell — rejected because scaling
cell coordinates overflows `Vector3i` for far-away doors, and a fixed scene offset from the door
never does.

### D4 — One portal camera in a `SubViewport`
The `SubViewport` shares the main `World3D`, matches the main viewport size and is rendered
only while a doorway is active. Its camera copies FOV/near/far and takes
`M_AB · main_camera` (outside) or `M_BA · main_camera` (inside). Cull masks keep each view clean:
city geometry is on visual layer 1, pocket interiors on layer 2; looking in renders layer 2,
looking out renders layer 1. Back-face culling does the rest: the portal camera sits behind the
inner door's wall (interior walls face inward) or inside the outer block (box faces face
outward), so the wall it sits behind is invisible to it. The door surface shader samples the
viewport texture at `SCREEN_UV`; inactive doorways show an animated shimmer instead.
The sun's `light_cull_mask` excludes layer 2; interiors are lit by emission and ambient light.

### D5 — Crossing in door-local coordinates
`PocketManager` stores the camera position in the local frame of the door(s) it watches. Rebasing
moves door and camera together, so local coordinates are unaffected and no false crossing can
occur. On crossing, the `FlyCamera` transform is replaced by the mapped one and its yaw/pitch are
re-derived from the new basis (doors are vertical, so the mapping is a yaw plus translation and
pitch is unchanged). `CityWorld` rebases on its next tick because the camera jumped 12 km.

### D6 — Streaming focus override (`CityWorld.streaming_focus`)
`CityWorld` gains `streaming_focus: Node3D`. Rebasing keeps using the camera (`focus_path`);
chunk loading uses `streaming_focus` when set. `PocketManager` sets it to the outer `DoorwayView`
on entering and clears it on leaving, so the city at the entrance stays loaded.

### D7 — Doorways are children of `ChunkView`
`CityWorld._load()` asks `PocketLayout.doorways_in_chunk()` and `ChunkView.build()` adds one
`DoorwayView` per doorway (glowing frame + surface quad), so doorways stream and rebase with
their chunk. `CityWorld.doorway_views()` lists them.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Double rendering cost] The portal view renders a second full frame while a doorway is
  within 160 m → only one portal at a time; a later change can lower its resolution.
- [No oblique clipping] Geometry between the portal camera and the far door could show
  through → avoided by construction (D4 culling, empty space around interiors); revisit if
  doors are placed on non-convex geometry.
- [Fog inside interiors] The depth fog uses the colour of the entrance depth, so deep interiors
  look darker. Accepted: it reinforces depth.
- [Float precision] The interior is 12 km from the door in scene space only while the camera is
  outside; error there is < 1 mm. Once inside, the origin rebases around the camera.
