## Why

Women were drawn as slightly shorter men, and every body had the same proportions. A crowd should
show women's figures and the full range of bodies: slim, athletic, stocky, heavy.

## What Changes

- Five body types (slim, average, athletic, stocky, heavy) chosen per person; heavier bodies grow
  more common through middle age, children are mostly slim or average. They change girth, limb
  thickness, shoulders, waist and, for heavy bodies, a belly.
- Women get a narrower waist, wider hips and a bust; about half wear skirts or dresses (with bare
  legs or tights).
- About a third of figures without a resident (gang members, wardens, escorts) are women.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `realistic-people`: new requirement for body types and women's figures.
