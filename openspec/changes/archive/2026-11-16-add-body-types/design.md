## Decisions

- **Builds as multipliers** on the existing girth, shoulders and height so every look stays a pure
  function of person and seed; the build roll comes after the earlier rolls so earlier traits keep
  their values.
- **Soft volumes**: bust and belly are wide, shallow ellipsoids that blend into the torso instead of
  separate balls, which read as buttons from a distance.
- **Skirts and dresses** are one shared truncated cone hung from the waist on the body, not the
  legs, so it stays put while the thighs swing inside it; a dress counts as part of the top so it
  turns into work clothes at work.
