# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Doorways can be walked through
A walking player SHALL be able to step through a magic doorway: the block's solid shape leaves the
doorway's opening free to 1 m behind the face, so the player's eyes cross the door plane and the
crossing happens. Crossings are measured at the player's eyes.

#### Scenario: Walking into a doorway
- **WHEN** a walking player stands on the bridge in front of a magic doorway at the bridge's end and walks forward
- **THEN** the player is moved into the pocket space and stands on its floor
