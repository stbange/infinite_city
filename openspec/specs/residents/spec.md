# residents Specification

## Purpose
Who lives in the city: households for every flat, persons with names, traits and memberships, door plates and the residents card.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Households fit their flats
Every flat — a non-core unit of a housing block, on every floor — SHALL have a household: vacant,
single, couple, family or flatmates, with 0 to 6 members. Between 3% and 15% of flats are vacant.
Every occupied flat has at least one adult (18 or older); children (under 18) live only in family
households with an adult at least 16 years older.

#### Scenario: Plausible households
- **WHEN** 2,000 flats are examined
- **THEN** every household has 0–6 members, between 3% and 15% are vacant, every occupied one has an adult, and every child has a parent at least 16 years older

### Requirement: Same residents every time
Households and persons SHALL be a pure function of the seed and the flat: two populations agree
and the order of queries does not matter.

#### Scenario: Two populations agree
- **WHEN** the residents of 300 flats are generated by two populations, the second in reverse order
- **THEN** every name, age and trait agrees

### Requirement: Names
Members of a couple or family household SHALL share a family name; flatmates have their own. The
naming culture depends on the location. Households of a block that share a family line share
their family name.

#### Scenario: Shared and varied names
- **WHEN** 1,000 occupied flats are examined
- **THEN** couples and families share one family name, and at least 60% of all full names are distinct

### Requirement: Person attributes
Every person SHALL have pronouns (she, he or they), three traits from three different personality
axes, a skill, wealth and health between 0 and 1, and at most one faction membership: a local
faction of their Reach that exists at the present year. Gang and society memberships are secret.

#### Scenario: Attributes in range
- **WHEN** 3,000 persons are examined
- **THEN** all attributes are in range, traits come from distinct axes, memberships belong to the person's Reach and exist at 517, and between 10% and 35% of adults have one

### Requirement: Door plates and residents card
Door targets SHALL be labelled with the household's family name (or "Vacant") for flats and with
the business name for workplaces. Interacting with a door SHALL show a card listing the residents
(name, age, workplace) or the business and its staff count.

#### Scenario: Labels name who is behind the door
- **WHEN** the player faces a flat door and a workplace door in entered blocks
- **THEN** the flat's label contains the household's family name (or "Vacant") and the workplace's label contains its business name

#### Scenario: Residents card
- **WHEN** the player interacts with an occupied flat's door
- **THEN** the HUD card lists every resident's full name

### Requirement: Fast enough
Generating all households of a block SHALL take under 5 ms.

#### Scenario: Households of a block
- **WHEN** the households of 20 housing blocks are generated
- **THEN** the average time per block is under 5 ms
